Article I

League Structure

§1.01 Name

This league is GridIron 24 — twenty-four franchises, two conferences, one champion.

§1.02 Conferences

  1. Detail Conference — twelve franchises.
  2. Overtime Conference — twelve franchises.

Each season, all twenty-four GridIron 24 franchises are randomly drawn into the two conferences (twelve per conference). Conference membership does not carry over from the prior season except by chance of the draw.

AAA League is a separate affiliated feeder and is not a GridIron 24 conference. See the AAA Rule Book.

§1.03 Conference Admins

After the annual conference draw is complete, the commissioner shall appoint one conference admin for Detail and one for Overtime. Conference admin assignments are set for that season and may change when conferences are redrawn.

§1.04 System of Record

ESPN Fantasy is the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, waivers, and transactions. GridIron 24 is the league headquarters for standings, rules, payouts, and championship presentation.

Article III

Rosters & Lineups

§3.01 Starting Lineup

Owners must set a legal starting lineup before each week’s games. Bench and IR slots do not score unless moved into an active roster slot.

§3.02 Injured Starters

Managers shall not start a player who is listed as Out, Doubtful, or on Injured Reserve. GridIron 24 Patrol monitors active lineups (daily pre-kickoff and automatically during the first quarter of NFL games) and issues automated warnings to the franchise manager. Questionable designations are permitted at the manager’s risk and are not treated as automatic violations.

  1. Each week a franchise starts one or more players in a prohibited injury status counts as one roster violation for that franchise, regardless of how many such players appear in the lineup.
  2. A third roster violation in the same season subjects the franchise and its manager to removal from GridIron 24, at the discretion of the commissioner.
  3. Staff may review and clear open violations in League Tools. Cleared records remain available for enforcement history.

§3.03 Single-Player Keeper

Each franchise may keep one (1) player from its roster into the following season. Keeping a player forfeits a draft pick in the round cost described below. A player may remain with the same franchise under this rule for a maximum of three (3) seasons (the acquisition / draft season plus up to two keeper seasons), after which the player must return to the player pool.

§3.04 Keeper Cost

  1. First keep — The franchise forfeits its draft pick in the same round the player was originally selected in that conference’s draft.
  2. Each additional keep — The forfeited round moves down one round (Round N becomes Round N+1). The franchise loses that later-round pick instead.
  3. If the calculated cost round is later than the final round of the draft, the franchise forfeits its last-round pick.
  4. Players acquired outside the draft (waivers, free agency, or trade of an undrafted player) use the final draft round as the first-keep cost, then follow the same one-round drop each later keep.
  5. Only one keeper is allowed per franchise per offseason. Declaring a keeper is optional.

Example. Josh Allen is drafted in Round 2.

  1. Keep him for the next season → franchise loses its Round 2 pick.
  2. Keep him again the following season → franchise loses its Round 3 pick.
  3. After that second keeper season (third season with the franchise), Josh Allen returns to the player pool and cannot be kept again by that franchise under this rule.

§3.05 Keeper Administration

  1. Keeper declarations are due before the draft date published on the season calendar (or an earlier keeper deadline announced by the commissioner). After that date, declarations are locked.
  2. Managers declare their keeper in Profile → Keeper. The keeper is highlighted in gold on the franchise roster.
  3. Original draft round and keeper-season count travel with the player on trades between GridIron 24 franchises.
  4. ESPN Fantasy is the system of record for the draft; headquarters and the commissioner administer keeper lists and pick forfeitures before the draft.
  5. Disputes over draft round, keeper clock, or eligibility are decided by the commissioner.

Article V

Playoffs, Bowl & Relegation

§5.01 Conference Playoffs

  1. Six teams qualify per conference.
  2. Playoffs begin Week 14 (Wild Card).
  3. Conferences reseed after each round.
  4. Conference champions are decided by Week 16.

§5.02 GridIron Bowl

In Week 17, the Detail champion and Overtime champion meet for the GridIron Bowl. Championship ranking uses each conference champ’s Week 17 ESPN score.

§5.03 Relegation

Relegation from GridIron 24 is determined solely by final conference standings at the conclusion of the regular season and conference playoffs, as reflected on headquarters.

  1. Within each conference, franchises are ranked by overall record: wins, then losses, then ties, then points for (PF).
  2. The franchise finishing last in each conference is designated the relegated franchise for that conference.
  3. If two or more franchises are tied for last place, the franchise with the fewest points for (PF) is relegated. The lowest PF among those tied occupies the relegation position.
  4. Each conference standings board shall display a relegation line immediately above the relegated franchise.

§5.04 Toilet Bowl

In Week 17, the relegated Detail franchise and the relegated Overtime franchise shall meet in the Toilet Bowl.

  1. The Toilet Bowl is a cross-conference score duel. Ranking uses each entrant’s Week 17 ESPN fantasy score.
  2. The franchise with the higher Week 17 score remains in GridIron 24 for the following season.
  3. The franchise with the lower Week 17 score is relegated to AAA League for the following season and is removed from GridIron 24.
  4. The resulting GridIron 24 vacancy is filled by the AAA League champion, subject to commissioner administration and the AAA Rule Book.
  5. In the event of a Toilet Bowl score tie, the commissioner shall resolve the outcome under league authority.

Article VII

Authority

§7.01 Commissioner

The GridIron 24 commissioner administers membership, franchise assignments, invites, the annual conference draw, and league tools for Detail and Overtime. Conference admins are appointed after the random conference draw (§1.02–§1.03) and may assist within their conference for that season. AAA League is governed by the AAA Rule Book and administered by the AAA league admin.

§7.02 Amendments

Material rule changes should be communicated before they affect active competition. ESPN setting changes that create conference drift are corrected through League Tools rules sync.
