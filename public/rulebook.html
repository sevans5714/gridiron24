Article I

League Structure

§1.01 Establishment

These rules govern GridIron 24, a twenty-four (24) franchise fantasy football league organized into two conferences of twelve (12) franchises each.

§1.02 Conferences

  1. Detail Conference — twelve (12) franchises.
  2. Overtime Conference — twelve (12) franchises.

Prior to each season, all twenty-four (24) franchises shall be randomly assigned to the two conferences, twelve (12) per conference. Conference assignment does not carry forward from the prior season except by chance of the draw.

§1.03 Conference Administrators

Following the annual conference draw, the Commissioner shall appoint one Conference Administrator for Detail and one for Overtime. Appointments apply for that season only and may be revised when conferences are redrawn.

§1.04 System of Record

ESPN Fantasy is the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, waivers, and transactions. GridIron 24 headquarters is authoritative for standings presentation, these rules, payouts, and championship determination under this Rule Book.

§1.05 Active Members

Active Members of GridIron 24 include:

  1. Dispatchers
  2. Janitors
  3. Prisoners

Article II

Season & Calendar

§2.01 Regular Season

Each conference shall play a thirteen (13) week regular season. Standings and playoff seeding shall follow ESPN results for that conference.

§2.02 Official Calendar

Key dates for the season are set by the league owner and the Commissioner and may be updated as needed. See the league calendar for current dates.

Article III

Rosters & Lineups

§3.01 Starting Lineup

Each franchise shall set a legal starting lineup prior to the commencement of that week’s games. Bench and Injured Reserve slots do not score unless the player is moved into an active roster slot.

§3.02 Prohibited Starters

No franchise shall start a player listed as Out, Doubtful, or on Injured Reserve. Questionable designations are permitted at the manager’s risk and are not automatic violations. Lineups are monitored for prohibited starters; automated warnings may be issued to the franchise manager.

  1. Each week in which a franchise starts one or more players in a prohibited status constitutes one (1) roster violation for that franchise, regardless of the number of such players.
  2. A third roster violation in the same season may result in removal of the franchise and its manager from GridIron 24, at the Commissioner’s discretion.
  3. The Commissioner may review, clear, or retain violation records for enforcement purposes.

§3.03 Keepers

Each franchise may retain one (1) player into the following season. Retention forfeits a draft selection as provided in §3.04. A player may remain with the same franchise under this Article for a maximum of three (3) seasons (the acquisition season plus up to two keeper seasons), after which the player shall return to the player pool.

§3.04 Keeper Cost

  1. First retention. The franchise forfeits its draft selection in the round in which the player was originally selected.
  2. Subsequent retention. The forfeited round advances one round later (Round N becomes Round N+1).
  3. If the calculated cost exceeds the final round of the draft, the franchise forfeits its last-round selection.
  4. Players acquired other than by draft (waivers, free agency, or trade of an undrafted player) use the final draft round as the first-retention cost, then advance one round for each subsequent retention.
  5. No more than one keeper is permitted per franchise per offseason. Declaring a keeper is optional.

Illustration. A player drafted in Round 2: first retention costs Round 2; second retention costs Round 3. After the second keeper season, the player returns to the pool and may not again be retained by that franchise under this Article.

§3.05 Keeper Administration

  1. Keeper declarations are due before the draft date published on the official calendar, or any earlier deadline announced by the Commissioner. Declarations lock after that deadline.
  2. Managers shall declare keepers through league headquarters. Declared keepers are identified on the franchise roster.
  3. Original draft round and keeper-season count travel with the player on trades between GridIron 24 franchises.
  4. ESPN Fantasy remains the system of record for the draft. The Commissioner administers keeper lists and pick forfeitures prior to the draft.
  5. Disputes regarding draft round, keeper eligibility, or the keeper clock are resolved by the Commissioner.

Article V

Playoffs, Relegation & Promotion

§5.01 Conference Playoffs

  1. Six (6) teams qualify in each conference.
  2. Playoffs begin in Week 14 (Wild Card).
  3. Conferences reseed after each round.
  4. Conference champions are determined by Week 16.

§5.02 GridIron Bowl

In Week 17, the Detail Conference champion and the Overtime Conference champion shall meet in the GridIron Bowl. The champion is determined by each entrant’s Week 17 ESPN fantasy score.

§5.03 Relegation

Relegation is determined solely by final conference standings after the regular season and conference playoffs.

  1. Within each conference, franchises are ranked by overall record: wins, then losses, then ties, then points for (PF).
  2. The franchise finishing last in each conference is the relegated franchise for that conference.
  3. If two or more franchises are tied for last, the franchise with the fewest points for (PF) is relegated.
  4. Conference standings shall display a relegation line immediately above the relegated franchise.

§5.04 Mayor's Cup

In Week 17, the relegated Detail franchise and the relegated Overtime franchise shall meet in the Mayor's Cup.

  1. The Mayor's Cup is decided by each entrant’s Week 17 ESPN fantasy score.
  2. The franchise with the higher score remains in GridIron 24 for the following season.
  3. The franchise with the lower score is relegated to AAA League for the following season and is removed from GridIron 24.
  4. The resulting vacancy is filled by promotion under §5.05.
  5. A score tie is resolved by the Commissioner.

§5.05 Promotion

The AAA Super Bowl winner is promoted into GridIron 24 for the following season.

  1. Promotion fills the vacancy created by Mayor's Cup relegation (§5.04), or another vacancy designated by the Commissioner.
  2. The promoted franchise joins the twenty-four (24) franchise field and is included in the next season’s conference draw (§1.02).
  3. Promotion is administered by the Commissioner in coordination with the AAA League Administrator.

Article VII

Governance

§7.01 Commissioner

The Commissioner administers membership, franchise assignments, invitations, the annual conference draw, and league operations for Detail and Overtime. Conference Administrators, appointed under §1.03, may assist within their assigned conference for that season.

§7.02 Amendments

Material amendments to this Rule Book shall be communicated before they affect active competition. Scoring or lineup settings that drift between conferences shall be restored to the uniform standard under Article IV.

§7.03 Final Authority

Matters not expressly covered by this Rule Book, and disputes arising under it, are decided by a league vote. A simple majority of the league pool — more than fifty percent (50%) of franchises voting in favor — carries the issue.
