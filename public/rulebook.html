Article I

League Structure

§1.01 Establishment

These rules govern GridIron 24, a twenty-four (24) franchise fantasy football league organized into two conferences of twelve (12) franchises each.

§1.02 Conferences

  1. Detail Conference — twelve (12) franchises.
  2. Overtime Conference — twelve (12) franchises.

For the 2026 inaugural season, conference assignment is set by the Commissioner. Beginning with the 2027 season, assignment shall be by conference draft under §1.03. Conference membership does not carry forward from the prior season except through that draft (including the MAYORS CUP switch under §1.03 and §5.04).

§1.03 Conference Draft

Beginning with the 2027 season, franchises shall be placed into Detail and Overtime by a live conference draft hosted at league headquarters. The draft is of managers into conferences, not of NFL players. NFL player drafts remain on ESPN under §1.05.

  1. Captains. The prior season’s GridIron 24 champion (league winner) and the runner-up are the two captains and Conference Administrators for the coming season (§1.04).
  2. Conference choice. The league winner selects Detail or Overtime first, is seated in that conference, and has the first pick. The runner-up is seated as administrator of the other conference.
  3. Schedule. The two captains shall set a date and time. The date is published on the league calendar. The live room is at the Conference Draft.
  4. Order. Captains snake-draft the remaining twenty-two (22) managers until each conference has twelve (12) franchises, including the captain. The winner picks first in round one; thereafter the order snakes.
  5. Board. Each available manager is shown with franchise (team) name, manager name, prior-season record, and prior-season points for (PF).
  6. Audience. Every GridIron 24 member may join the live room to watch. Only the captain on the clock may pick. The Commissioner may pick on a captain’s behalf if that captain cannot.
  7. MAYORS CUP switch. After the conference draft is complete, the prior season’s MAYORS CUP winner may elect to switch conferences. That winner takes the last-picked seat in the destination conference; the manager who was last picked there moves to the MAYORS CUP winner’s former conference. Conference Administrators may not be displaced by this switch. The election may be used once.
  8. The Commissioner locks the results for the coming season after the draft (and any MAYORS CUP switch). Results do not change the season then in progress.

§1.04 Conference Administrators

For 2026, the Commissioner appoints one Conference Administrator for Detail and one for Overtime. Beginning in 2027, the prior season’s first-place franchise is Conference Administrator of the conference that winner selected in the conference draft, and the runner-up is Conference Administrator of the other conference. Appointments apply for that season. The Commissioner may revise an appointment if a captain cannot serve.

§1.05 System of Record

ESPN Fantasy is the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, waivers, and transactions. GridIron 24 headquarters is authoritative for standings presentation, these rules, payouts, championship determination, and the conference draft under this Rule Book.

§1.06 Active Members

Franchise ownership priority for GridIron 24 runs as follows:

  1. Active members of the East Providence Police Department are considered first. For purposes of this section, active members include sworn officers and department personnel such as Dispatchers, Janitors, and Prisoners.
  2. Retired officers of the East Providence Police Department may be considered after active members, provided they were not an absolute piece of shit while they were here. Anyone who was a scumbag or a rat while wearing the East Providence Police Department uniform shall not own a franchise — and should play golf in a thunderstorm.
  3. If no suitable members are identified under (1) or (2), the Commissioner may go outside the agency to fill remaining franchises.
  4. Members of AAA League, regardless of East Providence Police Department status, who win the AAA League have the same opportunity to join GridIron 24 under the promotion rules in Article V.

Article II

Season & Calendar

§2.01 Regular Season

Each conference shall play a regular season whose length matches Detail ESPN (see §5.01). Standings and playoff seeding shall follow ESPN results for that conference.

§2.02 Official Calendar

Key dates for the season are set by the league owner and the Commissioner and may be updated as needed. That includes the conference draft date under §1.03. See the league calendar for current dates.

Article III

Rosters & Lineups

§3.01 Starting Lineup

Each franchise shall set a legal starting lineup prior to the commencement of that week’s games. Bench and Injured Reserve slots do not score unless the player is moved into an active roster slot.

§3.02 Prohibited Starters

No franchise shall start a player listed as Out, Doubtful, or on Injured Reserve. Questionable designations are permitted at the manager’s risk and are not automatic violations. Lineups are monitored for prohibited starters; automated warnings may be issued to the franchise manager.

  1. Each week in which a franchise starts one or more players in a prohibited status constitutes one (1) roster violation for that franchise, regardless of the number of such players.
  2. A third roster violation in the same season may result in removal of the franchise and its manager from GridIron 24, at the Commissioner’s discretion.
  3. The Commissioner may review, clear, or retain violation records for enforcement purposes.

§3.03 Keepers

Each franchise may retain one (1) player into the following season. Retention forfeits a draft selection as provided in §3.04. A player may remain with the same franchise under this Article for a maximum of three (3) seasons (the acquisition season plus up to two keeper seasons), after which the player shall return to the player pool.

§3.04 Keeper Cost

  1. First retention. The franchise forfeits its draft selection in the round in which the player was originally selected.
  2. Subsequent retention. The forfeited round advances one round later (Round N becomes Round N+1).
  3. If the calculated cost exceeds the final round of the draft, the franchise forfeits its last-round selection.
  4. Players acquired other than by draft (waivers, free agency, or trade of an undrafted player) use the final draft round as the first-retention cost, then advance one round for each subsequent retention.
  5. No more than one keeper is permitted per franchise per offseason. Declaring a keeper is optional.

Illustration. A player drafted in Round 2: first retention costs Round 2; second retention costs Round 3. After the second keeper season, the player returns to the pool and may not again be retained by that franchise under this Article.

§3.05 Keeper Administration

  1. Keeper declarations are due before the draft date published on the official calendar, or any earlier deadline announced by the Commissioner. Declarations lock after that deadline.
  2. Managers shall declare keepers through league headquarters. Declared keepers are identified on the franchise roster.
  3. Original draft round and keeper-season count travel with the player on trades between GridIron 24 franchises.
  4. ESPN Fantasy remains the system of record for the draft. The Commissioner administers keeper lists and pick forfeitures prior to the draft.
  5. Disputes regarding draft round, keeper eligibility, or the keeper clock are resolved by the Commissioner.

Article V

Playoffs & MAYORS CUP

§5.01 Conference Playoffs

  1. Six (6) teams qualify in each conference.
  2. Playoffs begin in Week 14 (Wild Card).
  3. Conferences reseed after each round.
  4. Conference champions are determined by Week 16.

§5.02 GridIron Bowl

In Week 17, the Detail Conference champion and the Overtime Conference champion shall meet in the GridIron Bowl. The champion is determined by each entrant’s Week 17 ESPN fantasy score.

§5.03 Relegation

Relegation is determined solely by final conference standings after the regular season and conference playoffs.

  1. Within each conference, franchises are ranked by overall record: wins, then losses, then ties, then points for (PF).
  2. The franchise finishing last in each conference is the relegated franchise for that conference.
  3. If two or more franchises are tied for last, the franchise with the fewest points for (PF) is relegated.
  4. Conference standings shall display a MAYORS CUP line immediately above the last-place franchise.

§5.04 MAYORS CUP

In Week 17, the last-place Detail franchise and the last-place Overtime franchise shall meet in the MAYORS CUP.

  1. The MAYORS CUP is decided by each entrant’s Week 17 ESPN fantasy score.
  2. The franchise with the higher score remains in GridIron 24 for the following season.
  3. The franchise with the lower score is the MAYORS CUP loser.
  4. Beginning with the 2027 conference draft (§1.03), the MAYORS CUP winner may switch conferences after that draft and replace the destination conference’s last pick.
  5. When AAA League is operating, the MAYORS CUP loser is relegated to AAA for the following season and is removed from GridIron 24.
  6. The resulting vacancy is filled by promotion under §5.05.
  7. A score tie is resolved by the Commissioner.

§5.05 Promotion

The AAA champion is promoted into GridIron 24 for the following season.

  1. Promotion fills the vacancy created by MAYORS CUP relegation (§5.04), or another vacancy designated by the Commissioner.
  2. The promoted franchise joins the twenty-four (24) franchise field and is included in the next season’s conference draft (§1.03).
  3. Promotion is administered by the Commissioner in coordination with the AAA League Administrator.

Article VII

Governance

§7.01 Commissioner

The Commissioner administers membership, franchise assignments, invitations, the conference draft under §1.03, and league operations for Detail and Overtime. Conference Administrators, appointed under §1.04, may assist within their assigned conference for that season.

§7.02 Amendments

Material amendments to this Rule Book shall be communicated before they affect active competition. Season scoring, lineup, and playoff-format rules lock at kickoff of Week 1 under §4.03. Scoring or lineup settings that drift between conferences shall be restored to the uniform standard under Article IV.

§7.03 Final Authority

Matters not expressly covered by this Rule Book, and disputes arising under it, are decided by a league vote. A simple majority of the league pool — more than fifty percent (50%) of franchises voting in favor — carries the issue.
