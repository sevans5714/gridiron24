Conference Playoffs

Weeks 14–16 play inside each ESPN league. Seeds #1 and #2 sit Wild Card weekend. Semifinals reseed so the top remaining seed always draws the lowest remaining seed.

GridIron Bowl

Week 17 is cross-conference. GridIron builds the championship from both conference champions’ ESPN lineups and scoring — no shared ESPN league required.

ESPN remains the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, and transactions.
