Conference Playoffs

Weeks 14 through 16 are played inside each ESPN league. The #1 and #2 seeds earn byes in the Wild Card round. Semifinals are reseeded: the #1 seed plays the lower remaining seed, and the #2 seed plays the higher remaining seed. Week 15 losers meet in a third-place game in Week 16 while the winners play for the conference title.

GridIron Bowl

Week 17 is scored by ESPN in each league. GridIron pulls the Detail champ’s Week 17 points and the Overtime champ’s Week 17 points and displays them head-to-head here. Set regular season to 13 weeks so conference playoffs are Weeks 14–16, turn reseed on, and make sure each conference champion still has a Week 17 ESPN matchup (opponent doesn’t matter for the Bowl — only their score counts).

ESPN remains the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, and transactions.
