Conference Playoffs

Weeks 14–16 are played inside each ESPN league. Six teams qualify. Seeds #1 and #2 sit Wild Card weekend. Semifinals reseed so the top remaining seed always plays the lowest remaining seed.

GridIron Bowl

Week 17 is cross-conference. GridIron 24 builds the championship matchup from both conference champions’ ESPN starting lineups and fantasy scoring — no shared ESPN league required.

ESPN remains the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, and transactions.
