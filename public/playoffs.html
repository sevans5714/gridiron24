Conference Playoffs

Weeks 14 through 16 are played inside each ESPN league. The #1 and #2 seeds earn byes in the Wild Card round. Semifinals are reseeded: the #1 seed plays the lower remaining seed, and the #2 seed plays the higher remaining seed. Week 15 losers meet in a third-place game in Week 16 while the winners play for the conference title.

GridIron Bowl

Week 17 is the cross-conference championship. GridIron scores both conference champions from their ESPN starting lineups — no shared ESPN league required.

ESPN remains the system of record for drafts, rosters, scoring, and transactions.
