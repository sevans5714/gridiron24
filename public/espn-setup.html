Create the ESPN league

Each conference lives in its own ESPN league. The conference admin creates that ESPN league so they are the ESPN league creator. GridIron 24 will still name it Detail, Overtime, or AAA on this site — ESPN’s title can be anything.

Open ESPN Create League

Who does this

  • Detail Conference Admin creates the Detail ESPN league (12 teams).
  • Overtime Conference Admin creates the Overtime ESPN league (12 teams).
  • AAA League Admin creates the AAA ESPN league (12 teams unless the commissioner says otherwise; AAA may be 10–14).

ESPN will not let the original league creator leave a slot. Whoever creates the ESPN league owns that creator seat for as long as the league exists. That person should be the admin who also plays in that conference.

1. Create a League Manager league

  1. Sign in to ESPN with your account (the conference admin).
  2. Go to Fantasy Football → Create a League.
  3. Choose League Manager (sometimes labeled Private / LM). Do not create a Public league. Public leagues cannot use our custom scoring, keepers, and playoff tree.
  4. Season: 2026.
  5. Number of teams: 12 for Detail or Overtime. AAA: 12 unless told otherwise.
  6. League name on ESPN: whatever you want. GridIron 24 will display Detail / Overtime / AAA from League Tools, not ESPN’s name.
  7. Finish create. You are now the ESPN league creator and occupy one team slot — that is expected if you play in this conference.

2. Make it viewable — not joinable

  1. Open LM Tools / League Settings.
  2. Turn on public viewing (anyone can look at standings and rosters without an invite).
  3. Keep joining invite-only. Strangers must not be able to claim a team.

GridIron 24 reads ESPN over the internet. If the league is viewable only to members, the site cannot load standings, scores, or the roster.

3. Match the GridIron 24 Rule Book

Detail and Overtime must both match the same Rule Book (scoring, lineup, and playoff tree). AAA scoring/lineup should match too; its bracket size can differ. Independent leagues set the full rules in their own GridIron HQ — they do not copy ESPN. After the ESPN ID is saved, League Tools → Rules Sync checks both GI24 conferences.

  1. Roster / lineup — same slots in Detail and Overtime (standard GridIron shape is 1 QB, 2 RB, 2 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, 1 D/ST, 1 K, plus the same bench and IR counts).
  2. Scoring — copy every Rule Book scoring value into both ESPN leagues. Do not leave ESPN default PPR/standard if the Rule Book is customized.
  3. Schedule — 13-week regular season in both leagues.
  4. Playoffs (Detail & Overtime) — 6 teams, reseed on, playoffs start Week 14, conference title Week 16. Week 17 is the GridIron Bowl on our site (ESPN’s “championship week” can stay Week 17).
  5. Playoffs (AAA) — scoring/lineup still match the Rule Book. Bracket size may differ (12-team AAA uses 6 playoff teams).
  6. Draft — Draft Day is Monday, August 24, 2026. Set the ESPN draft to that date once the commissioner confirms time. ESPN remains the draft board; keepers are declared on GridIron 24 HQ, not ESPN’s keeper tool.
  7. Save on ESPN.

4. Invite only this conference’s managers

  1. Invite the 12 franchise managers assigned to your conference (or AAA).
  2. Do not invite the overall site owner / commissioner unless they actually play a team in your ESPN league. If they are already ESPN creator of another conference, adding them here puts them in a second slot they cannot leave.
  3. Do not fill leftover slots with dummy ESPN accounts. Leave unused teams empty until a real manager is assigned on GridIron 24.

If you invite the wrong person as an ESPN owner, ESPN will not let that creator/owner seat be vacated. Fix it by creating a new LM league and starting this sheet over — do not try to “remove the commissioner” on ESPN.

5. Send this back

When the league is created, viewable, and settings are in, text or email the commissioner:

  1. Which conference: Detail, Overtime, or AAA.
  2. The ESPN league ID for this year’s league. Open the league office and copy the number after /league/_/leagueId/ in the address bar. Example: …/league/_/leagueId/123456789 → send 123456789. Do not send last year’s /league/settings?leagueId= URL.
  3. Confirm: 12 teams, League Manager (not Public), publicly viewable, invite-only.

The commissioner pastes that ID in GridIron 24 → League Tools → Sync → ESPN Leagues, names it Detail / Overtime / AAA on the site, looks it up, and saves. You do not need to rename it on ESPN.

Quick checklist

  • I created the league on my ESPN account.
  • It is a League Manager league, not Public.
  • It is 2026, 12 teams.
  • Anyone can view; only invites can join.
  • Scoring and lineup match the Rule Book (both GI24 conferences).
  • Playoffs: 13 regular-season weeks, 6 teams, reseed, Weeks 14–16 (GI24 conferences).
  • Only this conference’s managers were invited.
  • The commissioner has the leagueId.
