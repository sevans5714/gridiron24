AAA League operates independently of GridIron 24.

Article I

League Status

§1.01 Name

This league is AAA League — the affiliated feeder for GridIron 24.

§1.02 Relationship to GridIron 24

AAA League is not a GridIron 24 conference. It is not part of GridIron 24 standings, schedules, drafts, or championship boards. AAA and GridIron 24 operate as separate leagues with separate membership, administration, and headquarters.

§1.03 System of Record

ESPN Fantasy is the system of record for AAA drafts, rosters, scoring, waivers, and transactions. League headquarters on this site presents standings, rules, and payouts for AAA only.

Article II

Membership & Draft

§2.01 Membership

Final roster size is set by interest and shall be between ten (10) and fourteen (14) franchises.

§2.02 Draft & Player Pool

AAA conducts its own draft on its own schedule. AAA uses its own player pool — it does not share draft order, keepers, or rostered players with Detail or Overtime. GridIron 24 drafts and AAA drafts are independent events.

§2.03 Relegated Entrants

A franchise relegated from GridIron 24 via the Mayor's Cup (GridIron 24 Rule Book §5.04) joins AAA League for the following season, subject to AAA league admin placement and roster availability.

Article III

Rosters, Lineups & Keepers

§3.01 Lineups

Owners must set a legal starting lineup before each week’s games. AAA uses its own lineup structure administered by the AAA league admin. Slot counts and eligible positions are set in AAA’s ESPN league settings and shall match the published AAA standard for the season.

§3.02 Injured Starters

Managers shall not start a player who is listed as Out, Doubtful, or on Injured Reserve. AAA roster patrol runs daily and again whenever NFL games are in the first quarter. Violation policy for AAA is administered by the AAA league admin.

§3.03 Single-Player Keeper

AAA uses a single-player keeper: one keeper per franchise; first keep costs the original draft round; each later keep costs one round later; maximum three seasons with the franchise, then back to the pool. The AAA league admin administers AAA keeper declarations for the AAA draft.

Article IV

Scoring

§4.01 Scoring Settings

AAA scoring and lineup settings are administered in AAA’s ESPN league. Scoring or lineup drift shall be corrected by the AAA league admin. Official point values for the season are those published in AAA ESPN settings and this Rule Book’s payout / scoring notices when issued.

Article V

Championship & Promotion

§5.01 AAA Super Bowl

The AAA Super Bowl winner is the AAA League champion for the season, determined by AAA’s ESPN playoff / championship result as administered by the AAA league admin. The live bracket is published on the AAA Playoff Bracket.

§5.01A Playoff Field

Playoff berths scale with final roster size:

  • 10 teams — top 5 make the playoffs.
  • 12 teams — top 6 make the playoffs (current setting).
  • 14 teams — top 8 make the playoffs.

Standings display a playoff cut line immediately below the last qualifying seed. Seeds and matchups follow ESPN Fantasy as set by the AAA league admin; headquarters mirrors that bracket for members.

§5.02 Promotion to GridIron 24

The AAA Super Bowl winner is promoted to GridIron 24 for the following season.

  1. Promotion fills a GridIron 24 vacancy created by Mayor's Cup relegation or another vacancy designated by the GridIron 24 commissioner.
  2. Promotion is coordinated between the AAA league admin and the GridIron 24 commissioner, including conference assignment and franchise claim.
  3. AAA headquarters may display the current promotee for clarity only.

Article VI

Prize Pool & Payouts

§6.01 Buy-In & Pool

Each AAA franchise pays a $50.00 buy-in. The prize pool equals $50.00 × final roster size (expected range $500.00–$700.00 for 10–14 teams).

§6.02 Award Schedule

The award schedule below assumes a twelve-franchise field and may be adjusted by the AAA league admin when the roster locks:

AwardAmount
AAA Champion$300.00
AAA Runner-Up$150.00
AAA Third Place$100.00
AAA Most Points$50.00

Article VII

Authority

§7.01 League Admin

AAA League has its own league admin, separate from the GridIron 24 commissioner. The overall commissioner may appoint the AAA league admin in League Tools. The AAA league admin administers AAA membership, franchise assignments, invites, draft logistics, roster patrol, and day-to-day league operations.

§7.02 Amendments

Material AAA rule changes should be communicated before they affect active competition. Changes that affect promotion into GridIron 24 should be coordinated with the GridIron 24 commissioner.
